## Engineering principles

- **Start with the workflow.** Understand the people, handoffs, and outcome before choosing tools.
- **Make authority explicit.** Give agents scoped identities and least-privilege access; use deterministic controls for authorization.
- **Bind approval to the action.** Keep consequential changes under human review and enforce the approved scope at execution.
- **Verify the resulting state.** Check what changed in the destination system, especially before retrying an uncertain write.
- **Make evidence useful.** Connect the request, action, decision, result, and verification so another person can investigate and maintain the system.
